Help & Support

Welcome to the Help Center for the Kumbh Ujjain 2028 Smart Crowd Safety System. Here you'll find answers to common questions and resources to help you make the most of our safety system.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the Smart Crowd Safety System work?
Our system uses real-time data from sensors and cameras placed throughout the Kumbh Mela area to monitor crowd density, movement patterns, and environmental conditions. This data is analyzed to identify potential safety hazards and provide early warnings.
What do the different risk levels mean?
  • Safe (Green): Normal conditions, no immediate concerns
  • Crowded (Yellow): Elevated crowd density, exercise caution
  • Highly Crowded (Orange): High crowd density, consider alternative routes
  • Critical (Red): Dangerous conditions, follow evacuation instructions
How often is the data updated?
Crowd density and movement data is updated every 30 seconds. Environmental data (temperature, humidity) is updated every 5 minutes. During emergency situations, updates may occur more frequently.
What should I do if I receive a critical alert?
If you receive a critical alert, remain calm and follow the instructions provided. Typically, this will involve moving away from densely crowded areas toward designated safety zones. Follow directions from security personnel and emergency responders.
Is my personal data secure?
Yes, we take data security seriously. We collect minimal personal data and employ industry-standard encryption and security measures to protect all information. For more details, please see our Privacy Policy.
