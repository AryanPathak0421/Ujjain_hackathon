Privacy Policy

Last Updated: January 1, 2028

At Kumbh Ujjain 2028 Smart Crowd Safety System, we take your privacy seriously. This Privacy Policy describes how we collect, use, and share your personal information when you use our crowd safety monitoring system.

Information We Collect

We collect information to provide better services to all our users. The types of information we collect include:

  • Device Information: We collect device-specific information such as hardware model, operating system version, and mobile network information.
  • Location Information: When you use our services, we may collect and process information about your actual location to provide crowd density alerts and safety recommendations.
  • Usage Data: We collect information about how you use our services, such as the features you use and the time spent on our platform.
  • Environmental Data: We may collect environmental data such as temperature, crowd density, and movement patterns to enhance safety measures.

How We Use Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve our services
  • Develop new safety features and services
  • Provide personalized content and recommendations
  • Monitor and analyze trends and usage patterns
  • Enhance public safety during the Kumbh Mela event
  • Communicate with you about products, services, and safety alerts

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information to third parties. We may share non-personally identifiable information publicly and with our partners. We may share personal information with:

  • Government Authorities: When required by law or to assist in public safety operations
  • Service Providers: Companies that provide services on our behalf
  • Law Enforcement: When necessary to comply with legal obligations

Data Security

We implement appropriate security measures to protect against unauthorized access to or unauthorized alteration, disclosure, or destruction of data. These include internal reviews of our data collection, storage, and processing practices and security measures.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at privacy@kumbhujjain2028.gov.in.
